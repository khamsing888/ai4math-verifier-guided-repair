# Report Generator Skill

Use this skill when drafting, auditing, or compiling the final Master course report for PTIT INT4418.

## Mandatory Report Structure (Phụ lục C - 12-20 pages)
1. **Title & Abstract:** Problem, Big Data bottleneck, proposed method, key quantitative results.
2. **Section 1: Introduction:** Motivation, core research questions, the indispensable role of Big Data.
3. **Section 2: Related Work:** 3-8 recent literature citations, analysis of existing gaps.
4. **Section 3: Data & Ingestion:** Sources, licenses, schema, split distribution, quality metrics, data limitations.
5. **Section 4: Method & Architecture:** Distributed pipeline design, algorithm formulations, time/space complexity.
6. **Section 5: Experimental Setup:** Baselines, 3 scale tiers, primary/secondary metrics, hardware specs, seeds.
7. **Section 6: Results & Analysis:** Quantitative tables, scalability curves, latency-throughput Pareto frontier.
8. **Section 7: Ablation, Error Analysis & Threats to Validity:** Component breakdowns, failure taxonomy, limitations.
9. **Section 8: Conclusion & Future Directions:** Summary of contributions and potential paper extension.
10. **Appendices:**
    - AI-use statement: Directly embed quantitative tables and activity ledger from `docs/ai_use_statement.md`.
    - Contribution statement: Directly embed roles, PRs, and defense items from `contributions.md`.
    - Reproduction commands: Step-by-step commands to reproduce all baseline and scalability results.
